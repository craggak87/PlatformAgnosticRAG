# Safety Gate 2 - Perimeter Interlock System

## Purpose
Safety Gate 2 provides authorized personnel access into the robotic motion envelope of Cell A for maintenance, tooling changes, and inspection. It is integrated into the cell emergency stop and perimeter guarding architecture to enforce Category 4 / Performance Level e (PL e) safety integrity.

## Interlock Hardware
- **Switch Unit**: Pilz PSEN cs3.1 non-contact RFID-coded safety switch mounted on the fixed fence post.
- **Actuator**: Pilz PSEN cs3.1 unique RFID actuator tongue mounted on the swinging access gate door.
- **Guard Locking**: Solenoid-actuated spring-to-lock pin mechanism preventing gate opening until robot velocity has decelerated to zero.

## Electrical Connections & Safety PLC Inputs
Per electrical schematic [[E102 Safety Circuit Schematic]], Safety Gate 2 utilizes a redundant dual-channel safety architecture wired to fail-safe inputs on [[PLC01 Siemens S7-1500]]:
- **Channel 1 (Safety Input)**: Wired to digital input `I:0/10` (terminal strip TB1-21 to F-DI module Slot 5 Channel 0).
- **Channel 2 (Safety Input)**: Wired to digital input `I:0/11` (terminal strip TB1-22 to F-DI module Slot 5 Channel 1).
- **Test Pulses**: Test outputs `T0` and `T1` are generated by the safety PLC module to detect cross-channel short circuits.
- **Interlock Feedback**: Standard auxiliary door open signal wired to non-safety input `I1.2` for HMI status display.

## Operating Principles & Fault Behavior
1. Both dual-channel safety inputs `I:0/10` and `I:0/11` must be energized (+24V DC) simultaneously for the safety circuit loop to permit cell automatic operation.
2. If either channel opens, or if discrepancy between channels exceeds 250 ms, the safety monitoring logic immediately trips the master safety relay, invoking alarm [[ALM-205 Safety Gate Open]] and stopping [[ROBOT01 ABB IRB4600]].
3. Guard unlocking is commanded by the operator entry request pushbutton, which instructs the PLC to execute a controlled stop and release the solenoid lock once zero speed is confirmed.

## Maintenance & Adjustment
- Check actuator-to-sensor gap (nominal: 2 to 4 mm). Ensure gap does not exceed 5 mm when the gate is subjected to mechanical shake or door play.
- Verify mounting screws are torqued to 2.5 Nm.
- Perform functional test of both channels every 3 months.
